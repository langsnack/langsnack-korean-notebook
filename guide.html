WHEN YOU'RE STUCK

You don't need to think of a topic yourself

Use one of the random buttons in your writing workspace. The suggestion will automatically appear at the beginning of your writing so your teacher can see what you used.

↻ Random topic

Get something to write about.

Aa Random grammar

Challenge yourself to practise a grammar point.

“ Random prompt

Get a question you can respond to.

✦ Surprise me

Get a topic, grammar and prompt together.

Changed your mind? You can remove the generated starter and choose another one.

GET BETTER FEEDBACK

Tell your teacher what you need help with

You don't have to submit only polished Korean. You can leave notes directly in your writing.

AFTER YOU SUBMIT

Your writing isn't finished when you press Send

My Work

See everything you've submitted and check the status of each writing.

Teacher feedback

Open a reviewed submission to compare your original writing with the correction.

Discussion

Ask follow-up questions directly under the writing you submitted.

Saved explanations

Save useful explanations so you can find them again later.

BUILD THE HABIT

You don't need to write a lot.

A few sentences still count. Your writing calendar and streak help you see how consistently you're practising.

5 minutes is enough. Write something. Come back tomorrow.

Ready?
